Who we are

A family owned and operated business by Morton Makers.

We love creating. And we’re building a world where creativity and innovation can flourish and be encouraged.

Our belief

A child’s creation is more than a toy, a school project, or something made on a rainy afternoon.

It’s an idea they brought into the world.

We believe those ideas deserve a place to live, a story worth sharing, and a community that celebrates them.

Our mission

Morton Makers exists to celebrate children’s creativity.

We believe building teaches children to experiment, solve problems, think independently, and take pride in what they’ve accomplished.

But the most important thing we offer isn’t a lesson.

It’s a place where a child can proudly say, “Look what I made!” — and know that somebody is excited to see it.
